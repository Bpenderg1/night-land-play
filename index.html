The Night Land

The sun is dead. Humankind lives on in the Last Redoubt, a pyramid of light in an endless dark. You are a Night-Walker.

Far out on the plain stands an ancient installation. Reach it, wake it, and come back to the door. Something out there will notice you. You will not see it plainly. Bright light calls it. Darkness hides you, but then the way is hard to see. Answer your companion when called, and the bond will help you hold out.
